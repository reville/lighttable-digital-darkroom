LightTable 0.5.0 beta 1

Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) with macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1. Drag LightTable.app into Applications.
2. Eject this disk image, then open LightTable from Applications.
3. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to
   Security, and choose Open Anyway for LightTable. Confirm the prompt.

This beta is not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. A managed work or
school Mac may prevent opening it. Do not disable Gatekeeper system-wide.

Signed download and Mac App Store versions coming soon.
