Bring your Lightroom library

      
Start with a Lightroom Classic catalog.

      
  • Your originals stay where they are. Your Lightroom catalog stays unchanged.
  • Bring ratings, keywords, collections, and supported edits. Develop settings are approximate.
  • Close Lightroom Classic, then choose its .lrcat file. You’ll review what to import before starting.

      
      
Using cloud-based Lightroom? Export your originals to a folder, then choose that folder here.

    

    

      
Start with Apple Photos

      
Import the originals from your Photos library.

      
  • LightTable saves separate copies in Pictures / LightTable Imports / Apple Photos. Your Photos library stays unchanged.
  • Originals stored in iCloud will download. A large library may take time and extra disk space.
  • This imports photo originals, including RAW files. Albums, videos, and edits made in Photos aren’t transferred.

      
      
Whole-library import requires the updated LightTable app for macOS. You can also export originals from Photos and choose their folder.

      
Add new photos by running this import again. There’s no automatic sync.

    

    

      
Start with a folder

      
A few favorites or your whole photo archive.

      
  • Photos stay in their current folder. No moving or copying required.
  • Subfolders are included, so you can start with a single folder or an entire archive.
  • You can add more folders later from the Library menu.
